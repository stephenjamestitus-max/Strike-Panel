Is there a monthly subscription?

No. strikepane is a one-time payment of $99. You pay once and own it forever — no renewal, no monthly fee, no cancel risk. Ever.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — on any phone, tablet or laptop. strikepane is a PWA (Progressive Web App). Open it in your browser, tap "Add to Home Screen" and it installs like a native app. No app store required.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once installed, strikepane works fully offline. Your data is stored on your device and syncs automatically when you're back online — if you set up cloud sync.

Do my athletes need to download anything?

No. You share a link once. Athletes open it in their browser on any phone, fill in 6 sliders in under 60 seconds, and submit. No account, no download, no friction.

How many athletes can I manage?

Up to 20 athletes — more than enough for independent coaches, fight teams and small gyms. Full readiness scoring, morning brief, fight camp and weight cut tracking for every one of them.

What sports does it support?

strikepane is built for combat sports coaches: MMA, Boxing, Muay Thai, BJJ and Kickboxing. It also includes a Gym Mode for general fitness coaching.

Is my data safe? Who can see it?

Your data is yours. By default everything is stored locally on your device. If you enable cloud sync, it goes to your own private Supabase database — not ours. We never see your athlete data.

What is the AI Session Builder?

You tell it your training goal and it generates a full session plan instantly — rounds, drills, intensity, rest periods. It runs on Google Gemini. You bring your own free Gemini API key (takes 2 minutes to set up).

What happens after I buy?

Instant access. Check your Payhip confirmation email for your key, open the app in your browser, and you're live in under 60 seconds. Add your athletes and start logging check-ins immediately.
