FAQ
Common questions

Everything you
need to know.

Do I need technical skills to use this?
No. The app is a single HTML file — open it in any browser and it works immediately. No install, no accounts, no setup. The AI feature needs a free Google Gemini key (takes 2 minutes to get at aistudio.google.com, no credit card). Cloud sync via Supabase is 100% optional and only for coaches who want multi-device access.
Can I use this on multiple devices?
Yes. The app runs in any browser — phone, tablet, desktop. By default data lives in that browser's local storage. To sync across devices, connect a free Supabase project in Settings (5-minute setup, full guide included). Athletes can always submit their check-ins from any phone via a simple share link — no device setup needed.
What if I lose my data?
Use the Full Backup button in Settings → Data Management to download a JSON backup of everything. You can restore from that file on any device at any time. If you've set up Supabase cloud sync, your data is also backed up there automatically. We recommend exporting a backup weekly.
How does the athlete check-in work?
From the Morning Brief tab, copy your unique share link and send it to your athletes (WhatsApp, iMessage, email — anything). They open it on their own phone, slide through 6 readiness questions in about 30 seconds, and submit. Their score instantly appears in your Morning Brief. No app download, no account, no friction.
Is this really a one-time payment — no hidden fees?
Yes. $149 once, yours forever. No monthly plan. No "premium tier". No surprise charges. The only optional paid thing outside this app is Supabase, and their free tier is more than enough for any coach. The AI uses Google Gemini's free tier (also no card required).
Can a team split the cost?
Absolutely — and many do. One person buys the license, opens the app, adds all athletes, and shares the check-in link with each fighter. A team of 10 sharing costs works out to $14.90 per person. The app supports up to 20 athletes per installation.
What's your refund policy?
Because this is a digital product (a downloadable file), all sales are final. That's why we offer a full live demo — try every feature before you buy. If you have a genuine technical issue that prevents the app from working at all, email us and we'll sort it.
Does it work offline? Can I use the timer ringside?
Yes. Once you've opened the app in a browser, it caches itself as a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can install it to your phone's home screen and use it with no internet — including the boxing timer, which works perfectly ringside. Just keep the file saved locally.
