How do athlete check-ins actually work?
From your Morning Brief tab, copy your unique share link and send it to athletes via WhatsApp. They open it on their phone — no download, no account — slide through 6 readiness questions in 30 seconds, and submit. Their score syncs instantly to your Morning Brief. That's the whole flow.
Do I need technical skills to use this?
No. Open the app in any browser — it works immediately. The AI feature needs a free Google Gemini key (2 min, no card). Cloud sync via Supabase is optional — a step-by-step wizard is built in if you want it.
Can I use this on multiple devices?
Yes. Connect a free Supabase project in Settings (5-minute setup). All your data syncs across every device — phone, tablet, desktop. Your coaching brief is always up to date, everywhere.
What if I lose my data?
Use Full Backup in Settings → Data Management to download a JSON of everything. Restore it on any device. With Supabase cloud sync connected, your data is automatically backed up there too.
Is this really a one-time payment — no hidden fees?
Yes. $149 once, yours forever. No monthly plan, no "premium tier", no surprise charges. Supabase free tier is more than enough for any coach. The AI runs on Google Gemini's free tier — no card needed.

Does it work offline? Can I use the timer ringside?
Yes. Install it as a PWA from your phone's browser — it caches everything locally. The boxing timer, morning brief, athlete profiles and workouts all work with zero internet. Perfect ringside.
