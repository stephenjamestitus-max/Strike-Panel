# AI Image Prompt Pack — StrikePanel Marketing

Paste these into Bing Image Creator (bing.com/images/create), Ideogram.ai, or
Leonardo.ai (PhotoReal mode). Save results into `/marketing/` as
`boxing2.jpg`, `boxing3.jpg`, etc. — the Pinterest templates can then be
re-rendered against each background.

**Brand grade to aim for:** dark navy/black base (#02050a), teal accent light
(#00D4F0), warm gold rim light (#dfa84a). Vertical 2:3 if the tool allows
(Leonardo and Ideogram do; Bing is square — generate square, we crop).

**Hard rules (from past mistakes):**
- Boxing/combat sports imagery ONLY — no random people standing around
- No readable text in the image (we overlay our own)
- No recognizable faces of real fighters

## Workflow once you have images

1. Download the 2-3 best results
2. Rename: `boxing2.jpg`, `boxing3.jpg`, ... and put them in `/marketing/`
3. Commit, or just tell Claude they're there
4. Claude re-renders pinterest-trial v5/v6 (and any new layouts) against each
   background — ~2 minutes per variant

## Quality bar (reject if):
- Hands/anatomy look wrong (extra fingers — common with gloves off)
- Any garbled text appears anywhere in the image
- The lighting is flat or daytime-bright (kills the brand grade)
- A face is clearly visible and looks like a real person
