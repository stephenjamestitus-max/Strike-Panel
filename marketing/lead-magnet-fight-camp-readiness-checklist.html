The Fight-Camp Readiness Checklist

A one-page system to stop guessing who's ready, who's overtrained, and who's cutting too hard — for coaches running more than one fighter.

Free for coaches · Know who to push · Know who to protect

The fighter who says “I'm fine” is usually the one who breaks down mid-camp. Track these markers daily and you'll see trouble days before it shows up in the gym.

1Daily readiness markers (score each 1–5, every morning)

  • Sleep quality & hours
    Under 7h or restless 2 nights running = drop intensity.
  • Muscle soreness / DOMS
    Rising soreness across days signals accumulating fatigue.
  • Resting heart rate (on waking)
    +7 bpm over baseline = under-recovered. Track the trend, not the day.
  • Mood / motivation
    Irritability and dread are early overtraining tells.
  • Appetite & hydration
    Suppressed appetite during a cut needs eyes on it daily.
  • Bodyweight vs. target
    Log AM weight; compare to the cut timeline (section 3).

2The red / amber / green rule

GREENScores steady/high. Push — hard sparring, intensity, volume OK.
AMBEROne marker down or one rough night. Train, but cap intensity. Watch tomorrow.
REDTwo markers down or two bad mornings in a row. Protect — technical/recovery only.

Golden rule: two reds in a row = back off, no exceptions. Ignoring it is how camps end in injury.

3Weight-cut timeline (work backward from fight day)

8 wks
Set walk-around vs. target. If >8% to lose, start nutrition now — no late crash cuts.
4 wks
Gradual calorie/carb management. Weekly weigh-ins trending down ~0.5–1%/wk.
2 wks
Dial sparring load down as cut intensifies. Readiness scores will dip — plan for it.
Fight wk
Water-load → cut protocol. Daily weight + hydration checks. Never improvise here.

4Weekly coach review (15 min, every Sunday)

  • Who trended RED this week? Adjust next week's plan for them.
  • Who's ahead/behind on their cut? Intervene early, not in fight week.
  • Did anyone's RHR or sleep quietly drift? Catch it before they feel it.
  • Set each fighter's intensity target for the coming week.
