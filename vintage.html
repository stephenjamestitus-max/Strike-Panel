01 · Daily Readiness

MORNING BRIEF SCORING

Athletes submit sleep, mood, soreness, and energy scores each morning from their phones — no login, no app, just a link. One readiness number tells you who to push and who to protect before the session starts.

02 · Fight Camp

WEIGHT CUT TRACKER

Daily weight logs with fight-night projections. Know weeks out whether the cut is on track — or needs adjusting before it becomes a crisis. Camp phases, target weights, and cut timelines all in one view.

  • Daily weight log with target line projection
  • Fight-night cut trajectory visible at a glance
  • Phase labels: Base, Build, Peak, Fight Week

03 · Roster View

FIGHT CALENDAR

Live countdowns for every fight on your roster. Fighter name, opponent, event, and camp phase — your whole team's fight schedule visible in one place. Never miss a deadline or a camp milestone again.

  • Countdown per fighter to fight night
  • Opponent and event notes attached to each bout
  • Camp phase indicator: where is each fighter right now?
