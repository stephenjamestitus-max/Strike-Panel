# /follow

Find and follow relevant combat sports coaching accounts.

## Usage
```
/follow instagram 20
/follow x 20
/follow instagram,x 20
```

## Instructions

1. Use Zernio MCP tools to search for accounts on the platform
   matching these criteria:
   - 5000+ followers
   - Posts at least 2-3 times per week
   - Content is coaching focused not fighter focused
   - US or UK based preferred
   - Real engagement (specific comments, not just emojis)

2. Search these categories:
   - Boxing coaches and trainers
   - MMA coaches and gym owners
   - BJJ coaches and academies
   - Muay Thai coaches
   - Combat sports S&C specialists
   - Fight team accounts

3. Do NOT follow:
   - Pure fighter accounts with no coaching content
   - Highlight or news only accounts
   - Accounts already followed
   - Anything with suspicious follower/engagement ratio

4. Follow the accounts via Zernio MCP.

5. Default: follow 20 accounts.
   If user specifies a number, use that instead.

6. Output: list of every account followed with:
   - Handle
   - Follower count
   - One line on why you picked them
